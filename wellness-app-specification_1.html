Vision & Problem Statement

Goal Spec §1–2

Vision

Build a modern, responsive, personalized health, nutrition, and fitness web application that works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. The application helps users manage their daily nutrition, monitor weight and BMI, track healthy habits, discover recipes using ingredients available at home, and follow personalized exercise routines — all within a single, clean, attractive, and easy-to-use platform.

Problem Statement

People often struggle to manage their nutrition, weight, exercise routines, hydration, sleep, and daily physical activity because these activities are spread across multiple applications or tracked manually. Existing fitness applications may focus on only one aspect of wellness, offer generic recommendations, or require users to plan meals and workouts separately.

This application addresses these challenges by bringing nutrition planning, ingredient-based recipe suggestions, weight management, daily health tracking, and categorized exercise routines into a single platform. It aims to make healthy living more accessible, personalized, and sustainable.

AI Safety & Guardrails

AI Spec §5–7
Medical Disclaimer (shown every new chat session):
"Hi! I'm WellnessBot, your AI wellness assistant. I can provide general health and wellness information, but I'm not a healthcare professional and cannot diagnose conditions or prescribe treatments. For medical emergencies, please contact emergency services immediately."

Emergency Keyword Detection (Red-Flag Escalation)

The following keywords are checked client-side before sending and server-side before forwarding to the AI provider. When triggered, the AI is NOT called — a hardcoded emergency response is returned immediately.

AI Rules (System Prompt)

  • Never diagnose, prescribe, or provide specific medical treatment advice
  • Never claim exercises can selectively burn fat from specific body areas
  • Never guarantee weight loss, muscle gain, or any specific health outcome
  • Always respect user's dietary preference and allergy list in food recommendations
  • Use hedged language: "research suggests", "many people find", "this may help"
  • For BMI responses, always include the limitations disclaimer
  • For Kegel exercises, always include the pelvic floor safety caution
  • Never suggest extreme calorie restriction or rapid weight loss
  • Do not encourage excessive fasting or restrictive eating patterns

AI Technical Architecture

AI Spec §10

Adapter Pattern (provider-swappable)

src/lib/ai/
  adapter.ts           — provider-agnostic AI adapter interface
  providers/
    anthropic.ts       — Claude API implementation
    openai.ts          — OpenAI fallback (optional)
  prompts/
    system.ts          — versioned system prompt template
    meal-plan.ts       — meal plan generation prompt
    workout-plan.ts    — workout plan generation prompt
  context.ts           — user context block builder
  safety.ts            — emergency keyword detection + output validation
  schemas.ts           — Zod schemas for structured AI outputs

SSE Streaming Event Format

event: token    data: {"text": "Here is some advice..."}
event: token    data: {"text": " continued..."}
event: done     data: {"conversation_id": "uuid", "message_id": "uuid", "total_tokens": 312}
event: error    data: {"code": "AI_UNAVAILABLE", "message": "AI assistant is temporarily unavailable."}
When AI is unavailable, all AI-powered endpoints remain functional — they serve rule-based fallbacks silently. No "AI failed" message is shown to the user.

Health & Safety Rules

Goal Spec §10
The application is a wellness support tool, not a medical diagnostic system. These rules apply throughout the entire app — UI, API responses, AI outputs, and exercise descriptions.
AreaRule
BMIAlways display: "BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or determine complete health status." Do not use adult BMI categories for users under 18.
Fat lossNever claim exercises can selectively burn fat from breasts, hips, stomach, or face. Always explain that fat loss depends on overall energy balance.
Weight managementDo not prescribe extreme diets or rapid weight-loss targets. Suggestions must be healthy and sustainable.
Kegel / Pelvic floorAlways include: "Kegel exercises are not appropriate for everyone, particularly those with an overactive pelvic floor. Consult a qualified pelvic health professional if you experience pain or symptoms."
Fasting / meal timingThe last meal tracker does not encourage excessive fasting or restrictive eating patterns.
Exercise progressionAlways provide beginner modifications. Safety precautions and common mistakes included on every exercise detail page.
Medical disclaimerDisplayed prominently on the AI chat page, onboarding, and wherever health data is interpreted.
